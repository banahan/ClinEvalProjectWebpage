## Project Members  

__Paul Banahan__  

Paul is a PhD candidate in with UCD School of Computer Science and the Insight Research Ireland Centre for Data Analytics. Paul is also the Senior AI Research Fellow at the Mater Misericordiae University Hospital, the first role of its kind in Ireland. Paul has a background in healthcare AI innovation and is currently working on the use Generative AI in emergency radiology applications.

__Prof. Aonghus Lawlor PhD__  

Aonghus is an Assistant Professor at UCD who specializes in medical imaging analysis and artificial intelligence applications in healthcare. His research focuses on developing novel anomaly detection techniques for medical images, including MRIs, X-rays, and CT scans, with particular emphasis on improving radiologist-assisted diagnosis systems. He works closely with clinical partners to bridge the gap between research innovations and practical healthcare applications.


__Prof. Peter MacMahon__  

Prof. MacMahon is a clinical professor at UCD and a consultant radiologist at MMUH and has further specialist training in emergency radiology and musculoskeletal imaging. Prof MacMahon has an interest in applying AI in radiology practice. He has been actively involved in the adoption and research AI tools for everyday problems in clinical practice. He has lead the adoption of the AI tools at the Mater's Radiology department for stroke, pulmonary embolism detection, and fracture detection.

__Prateek Mathur PhD__  

Prateek is a Research Assistant at UCD and the Insight Research Ireland Centre for Data Analytics. His research focuses on medical imaging, with a particular interest in disease progression modelling and the use of generative methods to create high-quality annotated datasets. He is currently working with vision-language models for tasks such as visual question answering and text-to-image applications, exploring how these approaches can open new possibilities for analysis and interpretation in healthcare imaging.

__Dr. Molly Godson Treacy__  

Molly is a fourth-year registrar radiologist at the MMUH. She is working on the synthetic lumbar spine assessment project. This project aims to evaluate different AI architectures for image generation for the lumbar spine and for cauda equina syndrome. The outcomes from this study will influence future work on this topic

__Dr. Sadhbh Doherty__  

Sadhbh is a fourth-year registrar radiologist at the MMUH. She has been working on the explainable AI for ultrasound thyroid classification. The aim of this project is to provide additional clinical context for sonographers and radiologist to improve the categorisation of nodules in the thyroid.

__Dr. Jane Burns__  

Jane is a stroke fellow radiologist at the MMUH. She has been engaged with the in stroke progression forecasting and synthesis. The aim of this project is to provide additional clinical context for neurologists and radiologists to improve the triaging and access to time critical care during stroke presentations.
